1. FULL PROJECT DESCRIPTION & VISION
What is NebulaCore
NebulaCore is an interactive dynamic simulator and real-time multimedia synthesizer designed to fuse celestial kinematics, gravitational attraction physics, and harmonic sonification into an audio-visual experience running at 60–120 FPS.

The system allows users to interact with a cosmic swarm consisting of 5,000 to 20,000 three-dimensional particles governed by physical laws of central attraction, fluid turbulence, orbital forces generated by independent celestial bodies, and reactive deformations driven by microphone input, touch, gyroscopic sensors, or hand gestures captured via artificial intelligence.

Design Philosophy: The Beauty of Space Physics
Unlike conventional audio visualizers that apply simple Fourier transforms to 2D bars or pre-rendered backgrounds, NebulaCore simulates a living, persistent particle universe:

Spatial Consistency: Every particle possesses continuous 3D coordinates, inertial velocity, return elasticity toward archetypal cosmic shapes, and dynamic trail vectors.

Full-Immersion Interface: Control elements (HUD, parameter menus, physics modals) employ advanced Ultra-Glassmorphism techniques with auto-dimming on touch, allowing users to admire trails and light beams in real time while adjusting sliders.

Pure Colors & Saturated Neons: Chromatic modulation follows vocal and kinetic frequencies while keeping luminance strictly within the ideal sweet spot to preserve saturation and avoid the milky whitewashing typical of generic visualizers.

Main Use Cases
Live Performance & DJ Visuals: Full-screen or projector visual installation for concerts, DJ sets, and multimedia events, featuring immediate reactivity to vocals and bass frequencies.

Kinetic Musical Instrument & Sonification: An interactive sound generator where particle speed and density compose pentatonic melodies, polyrhythmic arpeggios, and harmonic chords over cathedral reverb.

Cosmic Meditation & Relaxation: A hypnotic visual environment for relaxation sessions, featuring programmable auto-cycling across 13 galactic configurations alongside generative melodies.

GPU & WebGL Benchmark: A high-performance test suite for evaluating instanced rendering capabilities, shader fill rates, and real-time physics calculations on high-frequency displays (Apple ProMotion 120Hz).

Interactive Museum Exhibit: A hands-free application for interactive kiosks featuring gesture tracking (hand open/close) and gyroscopic tilt control.

Pillar 1: Gravitational Physics, Celestial Spheres & Inertia
Singularity Central Attractor: Keeps the swarm compact and balanced at the center of 3D space 
(0,0,0), preventing uncontrolled dispersion into infinity.

Sphere 1 — Wanderer (Emerald Green / Fast Orbit): Autonomous celestial body with a 600-unit effect radius. Its force can be inverted by the user at will, ranging from repulsion (
+3.0) to deep gravitational attraction (
−3.0).

Sphere 2 — Spectra (Electric Cyan / Spectral Orbit): Second orbiting celestial body with asymmetric frequency, responsible for generating chaotic resonances and complex spirals within the swarm.

Elasticity & Shape Memory: Each particle retains its original shape memory (homes). When external forces cease, the swarm organically returns toward the geometric structure of the selected preset (Galaxy, Torus, Cube, Sphere, Wave).

Mobile Tilt Inertia: Via the DeviceOrientation API, physical device inclination (Roll/Pitch) exerts a continuous gravitational force calibrated to simulate cosmic fluid motion.

Pillar 2: WebGL 2.0 Graphics Pipeline & 3D Vector Trails
High-Performance Instanced Rendering: The entire swarm of 20,000 nodes is drawn in a single GPU draw call. Each instance continuously updates its rotation matrix, audio-driven dynamic scale, and spectral color.

New 3D Geometric Trail System:

Complete replacement of the legacy full-screen "fade-plane" trick, which used to darken and blur the HUD and text overlays.

Generation of a dynamic buffer containing hundreds of trailing tails composed of actual 3D vector segments.

Each trail assumes the exact chromatic-spectral hue of its leading particle and fades with exponential decay and additive blending into space blackness.

Built-in anti-teleportation filter: upon preset changes, instantaneous jumps exceeding 400 units are reset without creating unsightly diagonal lines across the screen.

Dual Cosmic Lighting: Primary warm/neutral directional sun paired with a secondary contrasting accent sun and volumetric ambient lighting.

Pillar 3: Pentatonic Acoustic Engine & 3D Spatial Audio
Harmonic Acoustic Synthesizer: Real-time generation of multi-oscillator waveforms calibrated with weighted odd harmonics and exponential decay characteristics typical of grand pianos.

Real-Time 3D Spatial Audio: The Web Audio API PannerNode module is physically attached to the 3D orbital trajectory of the Wanderer sphere, creating a rotating stereophonic movement around the listener's head (optimal with headphones).

Integrated Polyrhythmic Arpeggiator:

Metric Modes: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, and Triplets.

Melodic Patterns: Single Note, Ascending (UP), Descending (DOWN), Cyclic (UP-DOWN), 3:2 Polyrhythm, 4:3 Polyrhythm, and Chaotic Random.

Chord & Resonance Suite: Modes include Solo, Fifth (Power Chord), Octave, Major Chord, Minor Chord, and Cosmic 7th Chord, featuring a dedicated overtone intensity slider.

Convolution Cathedral Reverb: Deep spatial ambiance with real-time adjustable wet/dry decay curves.

3. RESILIENCE ARCHITECTURE: WEBCAM, MICROPHONE & SANDBOXED ENVIRONMENTS
In modern execution environments (sandboxed iframes, Cloud Run previews, privacy-focused browsers like Brave or Safari), direct hardware requests are frequently blocked by default due to:

Missing allow="camera; microphone" attributes on parent iframes.

Explicit user permission denial.

Absence of physical hardware connected to the machine.

NebulaCore v2.5 implements a bulletproof resilience architecture:

Microphone Auto-Fallback:

In the event of a SecurityError, NotAllowedError, or missing microphone hardware, the system neither crashes nor halts execution.

The Audio Pulse Rhythmic Simulator activates instantly: an internal oscillator generates virtual pressure pulses modulated to musical tempo, keeping particles active, reactive, and colorfully vibrant.

The HUD button clearly displays: 🎙️ PULSE: SIMULATED.

Always-On Holographic Radar Webcam Fallback:

If the webcam is inaccessible or MediaPipe fails to load neural network weights, the Picture-in-Picture window immediately launches a 3D Cyberpunk Holographic Radar Simulator at 60 FPS.

The radar renders concentric circles, scanning coordinates, and a rotating fluorescent sweep line, enabling users to direct swarm attraction manually via touchscreen drag or mouse cursor—permanently eliminating black screens and visual freezes.

7. STANDALONE OFFLINE EXECUTION GUIDE
Single File Execution:

Save index.html, NebulaCore_Mac.html, or NebulaCore_iOS.html into any local directory.

Double-click the file to open it in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Brave, Firefox).

No installation, Node.js environment, or NPM packages required.

Keyboard Shortcuts:

[SPACE] : Pause / Resume physics simulation.

[F] : Toggle Fullscreen mode.

[P] : Take a high-resolution PNG snapshot.

[M] : Show / Hide control panel and HUD parameters.

[C] : Enable / Disable cosmic auto-cycling.

[T] : Toggle accelerometer tilt control (on equipped laptops or sensors).
